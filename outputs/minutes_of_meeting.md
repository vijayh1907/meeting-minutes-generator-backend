### Key Discussion Topics
- **Q3 Forecasting Model Performance**
  - The accuracy improved by 12% since the last sprint, primarily due to new feature engineering for seasonal patterns.
  - Segmentation by apparel, electronics, and home goods showed varying volatility, with electronics being the most unpredictable during the back-to-school season.

- **Holiday Predictions**
  - Predictions for Black Friday through New Year's were discussed, incorporating learnings from previous years into risk models.
  - Current accuracy for the holiday period is about 85%, with challenges noted in post-holiday inventory management predictions.

- **Return Patterns**
  - Return patterns have become less predictable due to policy changes, with online returns peaking around January 15th and store returns being more spread out.

- **Consumer Spending and Sentiment Analysis**
  - Post-holiday consumer spending has been harder to predict, with some correlation to the consumer confidence index.
  - Early results from sentiment analysis show promising correlations with actual sales.

- **Technical Infrastructure**
  - The team resolved Docker container issues and is currently processing about 2 million transactions per hour.
